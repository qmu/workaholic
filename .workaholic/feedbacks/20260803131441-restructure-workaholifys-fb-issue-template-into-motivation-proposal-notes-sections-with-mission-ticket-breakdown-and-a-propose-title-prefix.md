# Restructure /workaholify's FB issue template into Motivation/Proposal/Notes sections with mission-ticket breakdown and a "Propose..." title prefix

/workaholify's handling of feedback ("FB") issues should adopt a fixed three-section template going forward. Section 1 is a Brief Motivation — a short statement of why the proposal exists. Section 2 is the Proposal itself, laid out as a nested structure of missions, each listing its ordered tickets underneath it (for example: Mission A with Ticket 1, Ticket 2 nested beneath it; Mission B similarly; and so on) — or, when a proposal does not warrant a mission or tickets at all, the section instead records only the reasoning as a standalone feedback entry with no mission/ticket breakdown. Section 3 is Notes, for anything else worth capturing. In addition, the title of every such FB-derived proposal issue should always begin with the prefix "Propose..." rather than any other framing, so proposal-origin issues are recognizable by their title alone.
