# Balance structure and prose in FB issue authoring

/workaholify's feedback-issue authoring has swung between two extremes. Earlier it produced issues with heavy, messy heading structures and extraneous filler text; as a correction it moved to plain unstructured prose paragraphs only, which turned out to be an overcorrection in the other direction, since some asks — multi-step flows, multi-item proposals — read much better with a bit of structure. The ask is to land in between: default to prose, but use light structural aids (a short numbered or bulleted list, or a small table) when they genuinely help a reader follow a multi-step or multi-item ask; avoid heavy or messy heading hierarchies, and avoid list items that do not carry real content (structure for its own sake). And when turning a Slack ask into issue prose, correct apparent wording mistakes in what the requester said and fill in gaps a standalone reader would need — prioritize readability and accuracy over verbatim transcription of the original message. This concerns the FB issue authoring template and style only, not the FB-to-merge lifecycle recorded separately from #192.
