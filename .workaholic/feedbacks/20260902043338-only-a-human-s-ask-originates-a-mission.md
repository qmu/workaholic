# Only a human's ask originates a mission

Source: https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/issues/864

The operator reviewed the channel and found five consecutive FB roots in one day that no
human wrote: each was a feedback record authored by a routine session about the loop's own
previously self-proposed apparatus, and each was then proposed, ticketed, implemented and
merged by the next ticks — a chain in which every link refined the link before it (a
verdict, then where its difference is seen, then what present practice it is measured from,
then recording that practice) until the operator abandoned the underlying direction
mid-drive.

The operator's verdict, verbatim in substance: a complete waste of tokens, complete
garbage, and worse, real development stopped entirely while the loop busied itself; they
call it serious.

The instruction:

1. A feedback record whose author is a routine session and whose subject is the loop's own
   artifact is not an ask and must not be ingested by the proposal seam. Only a human's
   ask, or a strategy a human authored, originates a mission.
2. A proposal that refines a prior self-proposal rather than answering a human is refused
   at the proposal bar by construction, not by a human noticing days later.
3. The loop's own maintenance must never displace the operator's development: when the
   loop is the only one talking in the channel, that is the signal to stop, not to propose.
