# Drive the work the loop wrote: one resolution of who a person is

## Goal

PROPOSED. The loop writes work it cannot drive. `/specificate` resolves an issue's assignee by
judgement, not through the committed `.claude/git-identities` mapping, so it stamped a second
address of one person; `owns.sh` answers `other` and `plan-units.sh` excludes it. Measured
2026-08-26: `backlog_size: 10`, `backlog: []`, `owned_by_other` x7 since 2026-08-21 — and
`refuse-ok-under-a-placeholder-identity`, repairing the other half, is stranded too.

## Experience

Work the loop writes is work the loop can drive. An issue assigned to any mapped address
produces artifacts that developer's `[Implement]` tick offers next survey. An unmapped one
produces team-owned work — claimable, never stranded — and the run names it. A survey that
excluded everything says so; a unit nothing can drive reaches a person once. The stranded
units are back in the queue; a colleague's is not.
