# Make the maintenance tick's channel presence help the work along

## Goal

The `🔎 Moderation` thread is read by a developer deciding what to do next, and today it
reads as a bot reporting its own internals: a global clause stapled to every question, the tick's
counters addressed to a person, three near-identical questions six seconds apart, and a question
stating a count without saying what the direction achieved.

## Experience

The thread reads as a colleague reporting on the work. Questions of one kind arrive as one message
naming every subject, each body carries the tick's own reading and nothing that is not about its
subject, and no rendered line describes the tick's machinery.

## Acceptance

- [x] Questions of one kind arrive as one message naming every subject, so three arrived
      directions cost one reply. (#20260903101320-ask-one-question-per-kind-naming-every-subject-not-one-per-subject.md)
- [x] Every question carries what the reader needs to answer it and no fact that is not about its
      subject. (#20260903101320-give-an-arrived-direction-question-what-the-direction-achieved-and-the-tick-s-reading.md)
- [x] No post carries a key, a step id, a counter about the tick, a repeated impairment line, or a
      promise no step must keep. (#20260903101320-keep-the-tick-s-internals-out-of-every-rendered-post.md)
