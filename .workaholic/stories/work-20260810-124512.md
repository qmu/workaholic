## 2. Motivation

`check-deps/scripts/check.sh` reports three version-binding conditions, and until this branch `/drive`/`/implement` §1 terminated the whole run `pending` on any of them before the survey ever ran. FB `20260810070110` already recorded a live developer correction for the narrowest case: the plugin's own scripts stay runnable via Bash from the checkout, and the safety hooks stay active, independent of whether the Skill/Command tool binding resolved, so refusing to survey at all was disproportionate. GitHub issue #338 asked that this correction generalize to all three conditions; this branch judges the two conditions differently rather than softening all three uniformly, because `loaded_version_behind_registry`/`registry_unreadable` protect against a different failure — a superseded binding silently changing the survey's own answer (the 2026-08-04 double-pick incident) — that a checkout-relative invocation does not fix on its own.
