## 1. Overview

Closed the mission `give-propose-and-ticket-a-diagnosis-first-discovery-pass` (issue
qmu/workaholic#374): `/propose` now runs a history-mode discovery pass before scaffolding,
and both `/propose` and `/ticket` apply a shared diagnosis-first rule so a failure-report
ask gets a reproduce-and-localize step instead of the reporter's fix adopted uninspected.

**Highlights:**

1. `/propose`'s workflow gained a new step 5 (Discover); every later step renumbered.
2. `## Open Decisions` lets `/propose` record an unrecommendable fork, never silently choose.
3. The diagnosis-first rule is stated once, in `workaholic:discover`, read by both commands.

## 2. Motivation

`/propose` shipped a ticket adopting a reporter's proposed mechanism uninspected — no
discovery step, no seam for an unrecommendable fork. Hand-corrected once already
(`52681f0`/`3172a65`), but the gap was general: neither command measured a failing
mechanism before designing its fix. This mission closed both at their shared root,
`workaholic:discover`, rather than patching each write step alone.

### 3-1. Give /propose a discovery pass and open_decision items ([42388a00](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/42388a00))

Inserted a history-mode discovery step into `/propose`'s workflow, between reading
constraints and dedup, and renumbered every subsequent step. Discovery runs inline in the
same unattended session (a single history-mode pass has nothing to fan out to, unlike
`/ticket`'s three parallel modes), and a fork it cannot recommend one side of is recorded as
an explicit `## Open Decisions` item the driving session must resolve rather than guess past.

### 3-2. Add a diagnosis-first rule to /ticket and /propose ([7df4daed](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/7df4daed))

Stated the diagnosis-first rule once in `workaholic:discover` as a `diagnosis_first` field on
Discover History's output. Both `/ticket`'s Write Ticket step and `/propose`'s Emit-the-tickets
step now open Implementation Steps with reproducing and localizing the failure when it applies,
recording any reporter-proposed fix under Considerations as a hypothesis instead of the design.

## 4. Outcome

Both tickets are archived and the mission's two acceptance items are checked (2/2). The
discovery pass and diagnosis-first rule are documented in `CLAUDE.md`'s `/ticket` and
`/propose` rows. `node scripts/build-plugins/verify.mjs` and
`node scripts/test-workflow-scripts.mjs` are green (2472 tests); the version was bumped to
1.0.156 and `outputs/workflows` regenerated to keep the built bundle in lockstep with the
renumbered `propose/reference/workflow.md`.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Resolving a "genuinely unrecommendable" fork before shipping it as an `open_decision`:
  the subagent-vs-inline discovery fork in ticket `20260811001223` was not actually
  unrecommendable once compared against why `/ticket` uses subagents (parallel fan-out) —
  a single-mode pass has nothing to fan out to, so it decided cleanly instead of being left
  open on reflex.
