## 1. Overview

A ticket whose every named mission had closed was offered by neither the mission path nor the backlog path — it sat in `todo/` while `plan-units.sh` reported a queue that read as drained rather than as broken. The survey now treats `mission_member` as a premise with an expiry: a ticket is excluded only while at least one mission it names is still active, and a repaired ticket returns to the offer carrying the closed slugs that used to suppress it.

**Highlights:**

1. `mission_member` is now conditional on mission liveness, tested ANY rather than ALL across the many-valued relation.
2. Liveness gets one owner — `mission/scripts/read-active-relation.sh`, a pure reader keying on the area, not on `status`.
3. The repair is annotated on the backlog row (`mission_closed`), never reported as an exclusion.

## 2. Motivation

The exclusion rested on a promise: a ticket carrying a `mission:` relation is dropped from the backlog because it will arrive inside its mission's unit instead. Only `.workaholic/missions/active/` yields units, so the promise expired the moment the last mission a ticket named was closed — and nothing noticed, because the survey kept reporting the reason that had been true when it was written. The measured cost (qmu/workaholic#382) was six tickets unreachable for weeks in one repository, five of them genuinely open work.

The defect's shape is why it survived: the queue did not look broken from outside. An unattended `/implement` surveyed, found the tickets excluded with a reason that sounded correct, and reported nothing claimable — indistinguishable in a cron log from a healthy idle tick.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Stop stranding a ticket whose missions have all closed ([73178942](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/73178942))

The reproduction came first: a throwaway repository with one archived mission, one active mission, and three tickets confirmed the stranded ticket under `excluded` with reason `mission_member` and absent from `backlog`. Localization then separated the two halves — the ticket loop excluding on a merely non-empty relation, and the mission loop enumerating `active/` only — and the fix changed the first, because the second is not a defect but the definition of a drivable mission. Liveness moved into a new reader beside `read-relation.sh`, whose contract four seams depend on and which is left untouched.

## 4. Outcome

A ticket whose missions have all closed is claimable again, and the survey says so in its own JSON rather than leaving an operator to infer it. The live cases are pinned in the other direction: a ticket of an active mission stays excluded, a ticket naming one live and one closed mission is still a member and is never double-offered, and a ticket proposed under a `status: draft` mission still in `active/` remains unclaimable — the safety property `/propose` depends on.

## 5. Concerns

### Abandoning a mission now grows the claimable queue

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** A mission closed `abandoned` surfaces its unfinished tickets as ordinary backlog, so an unattended `/implement` will pick up work a developer may have considered dropped when they abandoned the grouping (see [73178942](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/73178942) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/drive/scripts/plan-units.sh`). This is the intended repair — the work was never withdrawn, only the grouping — but it changes what a routine does after an abandon.
- **How to Fix:** Nothing in code. The runbook now names the behaviour so an operator meets it there; a developer who wants the work genuinely gone ices the tickets or drops the relation, both already developer-curated acts.

### The close path remains a candidate site, deliberately unbuilt

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** Closing a mission still leaves the `mission:` stamp on its unfinished tickets, which is what a developer used to clear by hand (see [73178942](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/73178942) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/mission/scripts/close.sh`). The survey-time fix removes the need, but the stamp itself is untouched.
- **How to Fix:** Leave it. Clearing at close time would destroy the ticket's provenance and make closing a mission a queue-mutating act; the stamp is now surfaced through `mission_closed` instead of being silently load-bearing.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Reproducing into a throwaway repository before reading the reporter's proposed mechanism kept the hypothesis separable from the diagnosis — the mechanism was adopted because localization supported it, not because it was written down first.
- Checking the fix against the test explicitly labelled its tripwire (`testProposeWidenedBatch`) before running the suite surfaced the real design constraint: liveness had to key on the area, because keying on `status: active` would have made every proposed ticket claimable before its mission was driven.
