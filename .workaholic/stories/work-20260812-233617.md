## 1. Overview

The smoke suite went red on the operator server and green in CI because a calendar date was
frozen into one side of a comparison whose other side derives from the run's clock. The
argv-ceiling fixture now pins its only commit to a `+00:00` instant derived from now.

**Highlights:**

1. Diagnosis measured before any edit: `TZ=UTC` kept the event, `TZ=Asia/Tokyo` dropped it.
2. Fixture-only — `scan-window.sh` is unchanged and `outputs/` did not move.
3. The suite is green under four timezones and with the effective date advanced up to 400 days.

## 2. Motivation

The failure looked environment-specific and was not. `scan-window.sh` resolves its window
cutoff with `git log --format=%cd --date=format:'%Y-%m-%d'`, which renders each commit in the
offset that commit itself recorded — so the cutoff is the run's own local today. The fixture
compared that against a changelog line frozen at `2026-08-12`, which meant every machine ahead
of UTC failed during the last hours of the UTC day, and every machine, CI included, would have
failed from 2026-08-13 onward. The operator server (JST) merely crossed the boundary first. A
gate that goes red on the clock is worse than no gate: it teaches its reader that a red line
might mean nothing.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Make the argv-ceiling scan-window fixture date-deterministic ([c14ec00b](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c14ec00b))

`testScanWindowOversizedCorpus` pins the fixture repository's only commit to a `+00:00`
instant 24 hours back — derived from `Date.now()`, so it never drifts out of the `"2 weeks
ago"` window — and writes the changelog event with that same string, so cutoff and event are
two views of one value. A second event 30 days back keeps the unchanged
`window_events.length === 1` assertion proving that the cutoff still filters.

## 4. Outcome

`node scripts/test-workflow-scripts.mjs` reports 2447 passed, 0 failed under `TZ=UTC`,
`Asia/Tokyo`, `America/Los_Angeles` and `Pacific/Kiritimati`, and a clock-advance simulation of
the fixed fixture passed at now+1d, now+2d and now+400d across three timezones. No assertion was
removed or loosened; the corpus is still sized from `MAX_ARG_STRLEN`.

## 5. Concerns

### The scan-window cutoff and the changelog writer read two different clocks

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** The cutoff takes the earliest in-window commit's *recorded* offset while
  `append-changelog.sh` stamps the reader's *local* date (see
  [c14ec00b](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c14ec00b) in `scan-window.sh`), so a
  boundary-day event written from another timezone can fall either side of it in `/catch`.
- **How to Fix:** Normalize both to UTC if anyone reports a missing event.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Reproducing the failure as a printed pair (derived cutoff next to emitted events) before
  editing turned a suspected platform difference into a one-line date comparison in minutes.
- Where a fixture cannot control the clock, controlling the *commit's recorded offset* makes
  both sides of a date comparison derive from one value without freezing either.
