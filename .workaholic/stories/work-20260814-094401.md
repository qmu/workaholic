## 1. Overview

The unattended executor gains a second routing input: a mission or ticket may now declare, at creation, that the work's real-world verification cannot run where a routine executes. A unit carrying that declaration is handed to a person instead of merged and announced as implemented.

**Highlights:**

1. `verification_handoff: <what cannot run here>` — declared on the artifact, read before the merge-policy table by the new `drive/scripts/verification-handoff.sh`.
2. `handoff` gains a second entry path rather than a rival outcome, so one 🟡 shape, one PR section, and one `pending` token still cover the case.
3. `/propose` can write the declaration (`--verification-handoff`), and the loop drill inverts its `verify-implement` expectations when the drill ticket carries one.

## 2. Motivation

Development requests increasingly arrive already knowing the routine cannot prove them — the credential, device, or third-party account the verification needs is not in the container. Such a unit drained its queue like any other, merged on the `review` route, and posted `🟢 Implemented`: a line that says the work was verified when only the code was written.

Reading the routing path settled where the defect was. `grep -rl "Quality Gate"` over `drive/scripts/` returns nothing, and `effective-policy.sh` — the whole pre-change route input — reads exactly one frontmatter field, `merge_policy`. The router was never wrong; it had no way to know. That is why the fix is a declared field rather than a smarter reading of prose, and why the declaration must exist before the drive: a run able to declare its own unit unverifiable would have handed itself the soft landing `handoff` is written never to become.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Hand off a unit declared unverifiable here ([339e4bd3](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/339e4bd3))

Added the `verification_handoff:` declaration, its reader, and the routing precedence that makes a declared unit open its pull request and stay there with a `## Handoff` section naming what could not be run. Wired the writers (`/ticket`, `/propose`) and the loop drill's inverted fixture, and resolved the ticket's Open Decision in favour of widening `handoff` rather than minting a fourth route.

## 4. Outcome

A unit nobody could verify here no longer merges and no longer claims to be implemented: its PR stays open, its claim stays standing, its finish line is `🟡 Handoff`, and the run ends `pending`. The declaration is writable only by the artifact's author, never by the run that would benefit from it.

## 5. Concerns

### The daily Handoff-state aggregation is not built

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** The ask names a routine that would aggregate every pull request currently in the Handoff state once a day; this branch ships the state and not the aggregation (see [339e4bd3](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/339e4bd3) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/drive/reference/routing.md`). Until it exists, a handed-off PR is discovered by whoever reads the Slack thread or the PR list.
- **How to Fix:** Decide it alongside the repository-versus-developer routine scope proposed for issue #451, since a third routine was refused on standing grounds and this would have to ride an existing tick.

### `verify-implement`'s handoff fixture is proven hermetically, not on a live tick

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** The inverted drill rows are covered by the smoke suite against a stubbed pulls endpoint; no live `[Implement]` tick has yet routed a declared unit end to end (see [339e4bd3](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/339e4bd3) in `scripts/e2e/loop-drill.sh`).
- **How to Fix:** Seed one drill issue whose ticket carries `verification_handoff:` and run `sh scripts/e2e/loop-drill.sh verify-implement <issue>` after the next tick.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Locating the defect by grepping the decision path (`fm_field` call sites, `Quality Gate` readers) turned "the router misbehaves" into "the router has one input" — a two-command reproduction that justified a field instead of a rewrite.
- Asking *who may write a field* by the direction of its risk settled cleanly why the unattended proposer may write `verification_handoff` and still may not write `merge_policy`: one withholds a merge, the other grants one.
