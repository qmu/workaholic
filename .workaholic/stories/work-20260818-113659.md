## 1. Overview

An unattended `/implement` run in a cloud container could drive at most one PR-unit per tick, and the obstacle was its own success: merging the first unit advanced `origin/main` out from under the detached checkout, so every later freshen refused `not_on_main`. `sync-main.sh` gains §1b — a detached, clean HEAD that is a strict ancestor of the base tip is fast-forwarded onto it — and the run can survey again.

**Highlights:**

1. The ceiling is removed by a fast-forward, not by teaching the survey to tolerate staleness — `current: false` still forbids `ok`.
2. Every other off-base shape still refuses `not_on_main` byte-unchanged, a **named** branch behind the base included.
3. Two tickets minted an hour apart described the same defect; they were driven as one unit, with each ticket's Open Decisions resolved on the record.

## 2. Motivation

`sync-main.sh` §1a was added on 2026-08-12 after ticks died before surveying with work queued: the cloud container hands a session a checkout parked off the base, so a checkout standing on the base's **exact tip** with a clean tree is admitted rather than refused. That proof was taken at a tick's *start*, where it is total. It stops being total the moment the run merges anything — the base advances, the checkout does not, and the same container that passed §1a eighteen minutes earlier is now one commit behind. Two consecutive ticks measured it independently (after PR #490 and after PR #492) and each minted a ticket for it, the second while driving something else entirely.

The cost is a throughput ceiling rather than a stall, which is why it took five days to see: a tick that merges nothing never hits it, and the second unit is never claimed, so nothing in the routine's finish line says why the run stopped. Since the immediate-merge route landed on 2026-08-11 the archive carries 41 PR-units across 26 UTC hours that saw any drive at all — 20 of those hours carried exactly one unit.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. A run that merges cannot survey again ([ae60f2c0](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/ae60f2c0))

Records the defect from the first tick's side and carries the fork that decides the fix. Its Open Decision 1 — may the script move a detached, clean, strictly-behind checkout? — was taken; Open Decision 2, re-deriving freshness inside the drive loop, was rejected for the ticket's own stated reason: a second freshness path beside the one the contract names eventually disagrees with it.

### 3-2. The freshen step refuses every unit after the first ([12331d10](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/12331d10))

Adds `sync-main.sh` §1b and the regression that proves it. A **detached**, clean HEAD that is a strict ancestor of `origin/<base>` is fast-forwarded onto the base tip and reported `ok: true` with `advanced: true`, `fast_forwarded: true` and `previous_sha`, so the caller reports that the checkout moved rather than implying it never needed to. `drive/SKILL.md` §1 and `drive/reference/survey.md` state the sequel beside §1a's reasoning.

## 4. Outcome

An `/implement` tick can now survey, claim and drive a second PR-unit across an intervening merge. The hermetic fixture models the discard the way the loop experiences it — `origin/main` advances by *archiving* a ticket — and asserts the merged unit's ticket is absent from the next survey; it failed five assertions against the unfixed script, including the measured `current: false`, and passes after. Full suite: 3017 passed, 0 failed.

## 5. Concerns

### §1b is a standing grant over the caller's checkout

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** §1a's header states flatly that moving the caller's checkout is not this script's licence; §1b takes it for one shape, and a licence is easier to widen than to withdraw (see [12331d10](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/12331d10) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/branching/scripts/sync-main.sh`). What holds it narrow is the **detached** requirement.
- **How to Fix:** Treat any future request to admit a *named* off-base branch as its own decision with its own measurement, never as an extension of this one; the suite asserts that refusal directly.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Reproducing against the **base** rather than the checkout is what made the fixture honest — asserting only on `sync-main.sh`'s JSON would have passed a fix that moved HEAD without making the survey correct, which is the property the loop actually needs.
- Driving two independently-minted tickets describing one defect as a single PR-unit kept the fix from being written twice; the duplicate mint is the failure contract working, not noise.
