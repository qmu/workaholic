## 1. Overview

A draft release note took a story's first sentence as "everything up to the first period", which cut inside backticked filenames like `check-version-bump.sh` and left an unclosed backtick — corrupting the markdown of every line after it. A sentence now ends at a period followed by whitespace or the end of the line.

**Highlights:**

1. Measured before choosing: 32 of the repository's 199 stories rendered with unbalanced backticks; the new rule leaves 0.
2. The cheaper rule was enough — the backtick-aware alternative the ticket also offered was not needed, and the corpus says why.
3. The residual case was the 160-character clamp, not the split, so the clamp now closes a span it cut open.

## 2. Motivation

The defect is worse than a short line: an unclosed backtick does not stay inside its own bullet, so one story's wording damaged a section it had no part in. It was also common rather than exotic — a sixth of every story this repository has ever written opens on a backticked filename, because that is how these stories name what changed.

The ticket offered two rules and asked for the cheap one to be measured first. That instruction is what kept the change small: the general rule would have needed backtick-span parsing, and the corpus showed it would have bought nothing.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Key Changes cuts a story sentence at a period inside backticks ([6dee7fcc](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/6dee7fcc))

The sentence split scans for a period followed by whitespace or end-of-line, and the two 160-character clamps — the story sentence's and the merge-title fallback's — are folded into one shared awk function that never returns an odd number of backticks, closing a cut span before the ellipsis.

## 4. Outcome

Rendered over `v1.0.179..HEAD`: unbalanced-backtick lines 1 → 0, no other line changed. Over the full 199-story corpus the two rules disagree on 41 stories and the new one is right on all of them, with zero abbreviation mis-splits. The renderer stays clock-free, network-free and idempotent.

## 5. Concerns

### The corpus measurement is a snapshot, not a guarantee

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** "No abbreviation is followed by a space in any first sentence" held for 199 stories on 2026-08-18 and nothing enforces it; a future story opening `e.g. the runner …` would split early, though still with balanced backticks (see [6dee7fcc](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/6dee7fcc) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/ship/scripts/draft-release-note.sh`).
- **How to Fix:** If one appears, take the backtick-aware rule the ticket described; the header records why it was not taken now.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- The ticket told the driver to measure the cheap rule against the real corpus before reaching for the general one, and that instruction is what made the change five lines instead of a parser — a measurement that costs one script is cheaper than the code it avoids.

## 7. Notes

The clamp fix came from a failing assertion that looked like a code defect and was a fixture defect: a 200-character unbroken token never opens a span, because the clamp trims to a word boundary and removes the token whole. The span has to contain spaces for the cut to land inside it.
