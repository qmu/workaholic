## 1. Overview

The `[Housekeep]` tick's `🔧 Needs a decision` post now names what kind of finding it
carries in its first line, instead of repeating `<N> pull request(s) waiting on a human`
whatever it found. The heading is derived from what the step already resolves.

**Highlights:**

1. A conflict finding and an un-run auto-merge no longer share a heading.
2. `` `stuck:<digest>` ``, both posting gates and the tick's frequency are untouched.
3. The `❓` shape was measured against the channel and deliberately left alone.

## 2. Motivation

Issue #513 reported that the housekeep notification reads as a fixed template and draws no
replies. Reading `#dev-workaholic` first showed the premise was half right: both `🔧` posts
ever carried a situation-specific second line under a first line differing only in `N`.

The same read answered the second question. Both `❓` posts named their two options as the
shape requires and neither drew a reply, so the wording is not what silence is about.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Make the housekeep check-in carry its findings ([386fb601](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/386fb601))

`step-stuck-prs.sh` derives a `headline` from the `blocked_by` vocabulary it already
resolves — conflicting with main, waiting on review, with a failing check, still in draft,
behind main, uncomputed, and `stuck: <kind>, <kind>` when one post covers several — and
both byte-identical copies of the `🔧` shape carry it. The report's aggregate-noise reading
was filed as its own ticket, since it belongs to a routine this one does not name.

## 4. Outcome

A reader learns the kind of decision from the first line, and two new fixtures assert that
the headings differ while each still keys on its own digest. The `📦` post rate — nine posts
to housekeep's four over fourteen hours — is queued as ticket `20260818214615`.

## 5. Concerns

### The design rests on four posts, not a week

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** The ticket asked for a seven-day read, but both shapes are two days old, so the census returned four posts in total (see [386fb601](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/386fb601) in `.workaholic/tickets/archive/work-20260818-213641/20260818202706-make-the-housekeep-check-in-carry-its-findings.md`)
- **How to Fix:** Re-read the channel once a week of history exists; the heading derivation is mechanical, so a wider sample can only add kinds, not contradict the shape.

### Nothing ingests a Slack reply back into the loop

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** Issue #513 wants a notification that draws replies, and a reply today is read by a human only — the loop consumes none of it (see [386fb601](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/386fb601) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/notify/reference/notifications.md`)
- **How to Fix:** Left unbuilt on purpose; an answer the loop consumes is a new mechanism and needs its own scoping, not a reworded shape.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Reading the live channel first narrowed the work: the "fixed template" was the heading only.
- When a post reads as uninformative, ask whether the information is already computed — here
  `blocked_by` had it, and rendering it cost no new read.
