## 1. Overview

The always-on mission lens (`hooks/mission-lens.sh`, UserPromptSubmit + Stop) is
retired: the hook, its registrations, its tests and every live-behavior
reference are deleted in one change; the plugin now registers no Stop hook.

**Highlights:**

1. The `Stop` key is deleted from hooks.json outright — mission-lens was its only registrant.
2. The resolve.sh absolute-path-caller test pin is re-pointed at `progress.sh`, so no coverage is lost.
3. Eleven live-behavior documents are corrected; historical records keep the eleven-missions measurement, re-voiced as history.

## 2. Motivation

The lens's two demonstrated jobs are owned elsewhere now: the finished-mission
discovery it performed by accident (eleven done-but-open missions, 2026-08-22)
belongs to `/moderate`'s `closable-missions` step, and the roadmap view lives on
bare `/mission` and in the executors' own surveys, which never read the lens.
What remained was an every-prompt status line about queue work the loop itself
drives — the addressed-to-nobody shape that retired the `🔧`/`📦` roots and the
`[Workaholic]` routine — and the only always-on hook reaching a code path that
writes (the 2026-08-04 stale-install outage was its measured cost). Four prior
narrowings tuned its volume; none questioned the surface.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Retire the mission-lens hook ([a01cb34e](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/a01cb34e))

Deletes `mission-lens.sh`, its UserPromptSubmit entry and the whole `Stop` key
from hooks.json, and its four test sites — the embedded mission-resolution pin
re-pointed at `progress.sh` so resolve.sh's absolute-path coverage survives.
Corrects eleven live-behavior documents and comments (the runbook's
`dirty_workspace` row re-anchored on `lib/resolve.sh`'s migration, the `terms/`
glossary edited deliberately) and regenerates `outputs/`.

## 4. Outcome

The plugin ships one always-on lens (the policy lens) and no Stop hook; no
script is orphaned, and no surface speaks unasked in an attended session — the
unclaimed-work offer survives on-demand only. Full verification green: 3523
hermetic tests, build/verify/validate-metadata, layout-doctor.
