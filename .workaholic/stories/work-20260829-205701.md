# Make the two executors agree about a proved-empty claim

## 1. Overview

`Claim Retirement` was green on every run while three proved-`superseded` branches stood on
origin since 2026-08-21. The cause is one term the earlier localization never varied: CI has no
configured git identity, and the claim verdict is derived **identity first**, so `superseded`
was never reached and the turn reported `count: 0` — byte-identical to a healthy, empty turn.

**Highlights:**

1. Reproduced offline: unsetting `user.email` alone moves the candidate count **3 → 0**
2. One shared rule decides who an executor may scan as, bounded by the committed mapping
3. The drill now varies the term it had always configured for itself

## 2. Motivation

The mission's own premise was that CI's silence and a healthy turn were one reading. Half of that
was already repaired — the per-unit reading answers `unreadable` and reaches the claim holder —
but the **silence itself** had a second cause. `lib/claims.sh` says so in its own comment:
*Identity first: a foreign claim is untouchable at any age, so its liveness never even needs
measuring.* An empty `_cs_me` therefore answers `identity_unresolved` for **every** claim before
ancestry, liveness or `superseded` is consulted, and `actions/checkout@v4` configures no
`user.email`. The earlier finding varied the **credential** and never the **identity**.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Let the retirement act re-derive superseded where CI runs ([82880646](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/82880646))

Adds `lib/runner-identity.sh` — the one place deciding who an executor may scan as — and one
line in `lib/claims.sh` (`WORKAHOLIC_CLAIM_IDENTITY`, an **override, not a default**, so every
container is byte-for-byte what it was). The two CI-side scripts re-derive as the claim's own
author, bounded three ways: only with no configured identity, only as an author the committed
`.claude/git-identities` mapping names, and only that author's own row.

### 3-2. Verify a unit CI never saw reads by its own name ([9d8b7589](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/9d8b7589))

Verified rather than rebuilt: `ci-retirement-turn.sh` already answers `unreadable` per unit for
all three, never `taken`, and `step-retire-claims.sh` names all three in `needs_agent`. The
existing sub-table already documents this exact case, so **no fifth word is added** — which is
also the mission's own third acceptance item.

### 3-3. Drill the two executors under no git identity ([87bdc433](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/87bdc433))

`verify-ci-retirement` gains three rows and a second breaker: the two readings must **agree**
over the same refs; a live claim and an **unmapped author's** claim stay undeletable under both
identity states; and the breaker removes the re-derivation and demands the no-identity reading
fall to zero — the production silence itself.

## 4. Outcome

The two executors agree: **3** candidates with a configured identity and **3** without, where it
was 3 and 0; the act now reaches its proof gate in the no-identity state; a live and an
`awaiting_verification` claim still refuse by their own word. `verify-ci-retirement` 17 rows /
0 failed / 2 proved breakers, `verify-all --kind hermetic` 32 drills / 0 failed, suite 5168/0,
`verify.mjs` clean. Acceptance 3/3 with an empty queue, so the archive gate closed the mission.

## 5. Concerns

### The three named branches are still on origin

- **Severity**: moderate
- **Description**: The mission's second acceptance item asks that the three branches be gone.
  They are not, and cannot be from here: the container's delete transport is refused by design
  (`branch_delete_failed`, the documented 403), which is the whole reason Act 2 moved executor.
- **How to Fix**: nothing further — they go when `claim-retirement.yml` next runs on `main` with
  this change merged, which is exactly the path this branch repairs. Worth checking on the next
  tick rather than assuming.

### The repair is none of the three the ticket proposed

- **Severity**: low
- **Description**: Each of the ticket's candidates fails its own step-4 bound: configuring
  `user.email` in the workflow (any value makes CI read every claim as its own), reordering
  `superseded` ahead of the identity gate (a **foreign** superseded claim becomes retirable), and
  resolving identity per claim inside the act (same collapse, and the candidate reader would
  still yield nothing so the act is never called).
- **How to Fix**: nothing — the ticket's step 3 asks for the repair to be argued in the story,
  and the fourth option keeps the shared precedence untouched with a bound the mapping enforces.

### The drill's candidate extractor was silently reporting one unit

- **Severity**: low
- **Description**: A greedy `sed` capture over the reader's single JSON line returns only the
  **last** unit, which hid the new rows twice while they were being written.
- **How to Fix**: fixed here (`grep -o`); worth remembering that the drill file has other
  single-line JSON extractions of the same shape.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Reproduce-before-repair was decisive: one term unset, 3 → 0, no time spent on a guess.
- The bound came from a **committed artifact**, so *CI owns every claim* is refused by
  construction rather than by care.
- The drill's own fixture was the blind spot — it configured the term under test.
