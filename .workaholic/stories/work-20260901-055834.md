## 1. Overview

`verify-merged-claim` proved that the claim oracle can *see* a superseded claim and never that the work such a claim frees can be *taken*, so it would have passed over a fully reverted fix. Three rows close that gap — one of them the drill's first breaker. The fix itself had already landed on 2026-08-27; this branch verified it and drilled it.

**Highlights:**

1. A breaker row that refuses a fresh claim over a **live** claim, so the behaviour row below it cannot pass vacuously.
2. `verify-merged-claim` was `unproved` in the register — it carried no breaker at all while reporting `pass`.
3. `main`'s `outputs/` was stale from PR #799; the mandated rebuild carries the repair.

## 2. Motivation

The 2026-08-26 supersession change shipped its survey half and its claim half a day apart, and the drill covering it followed only the first: four rows read `list-claims.sh` and not one called `claim.sh`. That is the measured shape of 2026-08-27 — mission `make-workaholify-converge-the-account-s-routines` was offered, named in `resurveyed[]`, and refused by both claim paths at once, reachable by no path at all — and a drill that stops at the reading is green while it happens.

The ticket asking for this was written 2026-08-26 and reached `todo/` on 2026-09-01, because the publication carrying it (PR #635) sat `clean` and undelivered for five days. Its own premise had gone stale in the meantime: steps 2-6 were already in the tree.
