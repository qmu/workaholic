## 1. Overview

Repairing the stranded-publication path earlier today delivered proposals that had been open for up to six days — and what they queued was work the loop had already finished, twice over. These are the two halves of that incident: telling a person before a stale plan lands, and reporting the same-slug collision it left behind.

**Highlights:**

1. Both seams **report and never gate** — an age threshold on the settle act would strand precisely the publications the `clean` widening exists to deliver.
2. `create.sh` already refuses a duplicate slug and provably cannot reach this case: the collision arrives by **merge**, where no write seam runs.
3. Neither mission copy is deleted or chosen between; the operator rules.

## 2. Motivation

`publish-tree-pr.sh` auto-merges on opening, so a proposal is normally written and landed minutes apart and its age says nothing. Only one the transport refused stays open long enough for its plan to go stale — and until this week none did. Measured 2026-09-01: five of six open publications read `clean`, the oldest six days old, and landing them queued roughly fifteen tickets across two missions for work already on the base.

Nothing checked whether a proposal was still worth landing, and nothing noticed that the two missions it queued collided with missions already archived. Both gaps are consequences of the repair, not arguments against it.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Check a stranded proposal is still worth landing ([d792ae16](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/d792ae16))

`lib/publication-age.sh` becomes the one age derivation, shared by `publication-effect.sh`, `list-stranded-publications.sh` and `settle-stranded-publication.sh`; `/moderate` gains `stranded-publication-stale:<number>`, asking a publication's author whether the plan it carries is still wanted. The act stays unconditional.

### 3-2. Collapse two missions that plan the same ask ([3e67bc2c](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/3e67bc2c))

`layout-doctor.sh` reports each slug present in both `missions/active/` and `missions/archive/` as an advisory naming both areas, deleting and choosing nothing.

## 4. Outcome

A stale plan can no longer land with nobody told, and the two same-slug pairs already in the tree are named where the operator will meet them. Neither change can stop, hold or drop anything: the settle act behaves identically over a six-day-old publication and a six-minute-old one, and the audit stays `conforming: true`.

## 5. Concerns

### Two mission slugs each name two records, awaiting the operator's ruling

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** `deliver-what-the-loop-already-knows-to-the-person-who-can-act` and `say-when-the-loop-has-run-out-of-direction` each exist in both `missions/active/` and `missions/archive/`. Both copies are history — the archived ones carry the implementation changelogs and `actual_hours`, the active ones the verification changelogs written earlier today. The collision bites when something tries to move an active copy into an occupied archive (see [3e67bc2c](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/3e67bc2c) in `plugins/workaholic/hooks/layout-doctor.sh`).
- **How to Fix:** The operator rules which record survives. The advisory names both and says *delete neither*; nothing automated will act on it.

### `/specificate` can still write two feedback records for one ask

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** This is the root cause of both pairs — the dedup keys on the ask's feedback refs, and two records for one ask carry different ones, so the duplicate is invisible while the slug collides. This change reports the result; it does not stop the second record being written.
- **How to Fix:** Not attempted here — it is `/specificate`'s dedup, a different skill and a different ask, and the audit now makes every instance visible. Worth a ticket once the operator has ruled on the two existing pairs, since the ruling will show whether a second record is ever legitimate.

### `scripts/e2e/loop-drill.sh` remains over the per-file size ceiling

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** 584,123 bytes against 524,288 — the branch's only scan finding, `size`/`override`, unchanged by this branch and already raised on two earlier branches today.
- **How to Fix:** Split the dispatcher from the `cmd_verify_*` bodies.
