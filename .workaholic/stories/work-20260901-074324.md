## 1. Overview

`main` carries two `mission.md` files sharing one slug. This branch established how, from
git rather than from inference, and rewrote the ticket to match: the cause is a publish
tree's collision check going stale while its publication sat unmerged for six days, not the
two seams the ticket originally blamed.

**Highlights:**

1. The collision is traced to PR #625 merging six days after its tree was built.
2. Two of the ticket's premises were disproved by reading the scripts it named.
3. The ticket stays queued on an operator ruling, now with a correct diagnosis.

## 2. Motivation

The collision was found while driving an unrelated mission: its archive gate proved the
mission `achieved` and then could not move the record, because the destination was already
occupied. A ticket was minted from that observation and, being minted mid-run, it was
written from what the archive step had shown rather than from the scripts themselves.

Driving it disproved it. That is the useful outcome: a ticket that sends someone to repair
`create.sh`'s area coverage and `close.sh`'s conflict answer would have cost that person the
same reading, and left two working mechanisms churned.

## 5. Concerns

### The two records still both sit on main

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** `.workaholic/missions/active/say-when-the-loop-has-run-out-of-direction/mission.md` reads `status: achieved` while sitting in the surveyed `active/` area, beside an identically-slugged record in `archive/`. Every slug-keyed reader composes `mission_resolve`, which searches `active/` then `archive/` and returns the first hit, so downstream readings are area-order dependent until this is settled.
- **How to Fix:** Rule on the ticket's Open Decision, then apply the chosen disposition. Nothing should be deleted or re-slugged before that ruling.

### A stranded publication can carry an arbitrarily stale write-time check

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** This collision is one instance of a general property: any check a publish tree performs against its base is only as fresh as the moment the tree was built, and nothing re-checks at merge. PR #625 merged six days after its check ran (see [c5182f7](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c5182f74) in `.workaholic/tickets/todo/20260901072600-rule-on-two-missions-sharing-one-slug.md`).
- **How to Fix:** The ticket's step 2 sets out the two candidate places to close it. The broader question — whether other publish-tree checks share the property — is worth asking once the slug case is settled.
