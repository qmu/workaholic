## 1. Overview

*The language of a post is the language its readers use* (2026-09-01) settled **which** language a
post is written in. It said nothing about **what good Japanese is**, and the operator has been
receiving the consequence: posts translated from the English record title word for word, with
English word order kept around them.

**Highlights:**

1. The bar is stated as an **outcome a reader can check** — *a channel reader must understand what
   is being asked without opening the English record behind the link* — not as a style preference.
2. It ships in **one wording** across five surfaces and is pinned **byte-identical**, on the same
   ground the post formats already are.
3. The drift pin is **demonstrated, not asserted**: editing one ceiling's clause alone turns the
   suite red.

## 2. Motivation

The measured posts: 「組み立てを止める」 for *fail the build* — a reader cannot tell it means CI — a bare
「形」 for *shape*, and 「示せるという判定」 for *demonstrable verdict*. Each is a word-for-word rendering
of an English title, and each costs the reader the one thing the post exists to give them.

The rule's own 2026-09-01 measurement is why the bar belongs beside the shapes rather than in a
repository's `CLAUDE.md`: a consuming repository *did* carry the Japanese rule and still got an
English root, because the ceiling names the only shapes a session may emit and shows every one of
them in English. **A ceiling that shows a language is a ceiling that sets one** — and the same
ceiling is what will set the register.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. The bar, in the rule's own home

`rules/interaction.md` gains the outcome, the three rules that follow from it (established
technical terms keep their katakana or English form; a title's **meaning** is translated, never its
words; a title that resists translation is paraphrased rather than transliterated), the three
measured calques as worked repairs, and — in its own paragraph — the limit.

### 3-2. Carried byte-identically to the five surfaces that instruct a session

The four routine-fired ceilings already shared **one** byte-identical sentence stating the language
rule, so the bar was carried by substitution on that anchor rather than written four times. It also
sits in the notify catalog directly under the paragraph that already says its English is the
instruction rather than the wire text — beside the fenced blocks a session actually copies.

### 3-3. The drift pin, proved by breaking it

The existing language-rule assertion gained a byte-identical comparison across the four ceilings
and the catalog, three assertions on the rule's own home, and one on the stated limit.
`rules/interaction.md` is deliberately **not** held to the identical string: `rules/` carries the
measurement and the why, a ceiling carries the instruction, and pinning them identical would force
the reasoning into four places that do not want it.

## 4. Outcome

Every surface a session reads what to emit from now carries the same bar in the same words, and a
future edit to one of them alone fails the suite. `node scripts/test-workflow-scripts.mjs` →
**5976 passed, 0 failed**; `outputs/` regenerated with no diff; `verify.mjs`,
`validate-metadata.mjs` and `layout-doctor.sh` all clean.

## 5. Concerns

### The bar is prose, and nothing checks the Japanese a run emits

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** The composition happens at run time and never appears in this tree, so the pin
  proves only that the bar is *stated* where a session reads. A run can carry the rule in context
  and still post a calque, and nothing here would notice.
- **How to Fix:** Not fixable in this direction — the rule now says so in its own words rather than
  implying otherwise. What would notice is a person reading the channel, which is the same loop the
  three measured examples came through.

### Three worked pairs is a small sample of one failure mode

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** All three measured calques are noun-phrase renderings of English titles. Word
  order, which the ticket names as part of the defect, has no worked example, so a post with
  correct vocabulary and English syntax is not directly covered by an example.
- **How to Fix:** Add a pair when one is measured. The outcome test — can a reader understand it
  without opening the record — already covers the case; only the illustration is missing.

## 8. Notes

Carrying the clause by substitution on an existing byte-identical anchor is worth recording as a
technique: it made "one wording, five surfaces" a property of how the edit was made rather than
something the pin had to catch afterwards.
