## 1. Overview

Three missions read `status: active` with `0/3` acceptance and 17 queued tickets between them while the behaviour each asked for was already on `main` — put there by a person's own single commit, with the loop's own pull request closed unmerged. Nothing had recorded the work, so nothing ticked the acceptance and the survey was queued to re-implement `main`. This branch adds the `unrecorded-missions` tick step that names such a mission to its assignee.

**Highlights:**

1. Four tree terms gate one bounded pull-request read; only a `closed_unmerged` branch is a candidate.
2. It closes nothing and excludes nothing — what it establishes is that nothing *recorded* the work, never that the work is undone.
3. The measured missions are still named by nobody, and the step says so by name rather than guessing a branch for them.

## 2. Motivation

`closable-missions` proves a mission finished by arithmetic — `checked == total`, `unlinked == 0`, an empty queue. These missions are its photographic negative and satisfy none of it: acceptance untouched, queue full, and yet finished in every sense a reader cares about. Drivability is derived from *active area + plan + queued tickets*, and all three terms still held, so the executor kept offering work that `main` already had. The gap is not a gate failing — the claim protocol held one claim per unit throughout — it is that a person implementing a filed finding by hand is a route the loop cannot see, and nothing looked at what happens afterwards.

### 3-1. Close a mission whose work landed by another route ([54f0d85](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/54f0d855))

Adds `step-unrecorded-missions.sh`: active, acceptance entirely unticked, no archived ticket in the mission's own `## Changelog`, and a non-empty queue — then one `branch-pull-request-state.sh` read, with `closed_unmerged` the only candidate. The mission's assignee is asked once under `unrecorded-mission:<slug>` to close it or drive it again.

## 4. Outcome

The tick now has a reading for the one mission state that was invisible to every other step, and it delivers that reading as a question rather than an act. Registered in `run.sh`, in `moderate/reference/workflow.md` (§12a, `needs_ruling`, and the transport-derived-count table) and in `CLAUDE.md`'s step table, with nine hermetic rows that stub the transport rather than the step.

## 5. Concerns

### The three measured missions are named by nobody

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** `claim.sh` writes a mission's `claim:` field **on the claim branch**, so a branch closed unmerged never carries it to the base — all three measured missions read `active` with no `claim:` line — and their branches have since been deleted by the `pull_request_closed_unmerged` retirement this same mission shipped. Neither branch source resolves, so the step counts them `claim_branch_unresolved` (see [54f0d85](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/54f0d855) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/moderate/scripts/step-unrecorded-missions.sh`). Verified live: `6 whose claim branch neither the mission nor the claim scan names`.
- **How to Fix:** Rule on the three by hand (`/mission-close <slug> abandoned`, or re-drive them), and decide separately whether the claim→mission link should be recorded at claim time on a ref that survives the branch's deletion.

### The retirement and this reading are in tension by construction

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** deleting a `closed_unmerged` branch is right — it can never merge — and it destroys the last evidence linking that branch to its mission; both shipped on this mission (see [54f0d85](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/54f0d855) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/moderate/scripts/step-unrecorded-missions.sh`).
- **How to Fix:** If the link is worth keeping, record it where the deletion cannot reach it; until somebody rules on that, the step's `claim_branch_unresolved` count is the honest answer.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- **Stub the transport, not the step** — every tree term stayed under test for real while the row remained hermetic, and the fixture states its meaning in the branch name.
- **When a ticket's stated term cannot be read, add a second source and name the residue** — the `claim:` field is right when it exists, the claim oracle answers when it does not, and the case neither answers is counted rather than guessed at.
