## 1. Overview

`🟢 Implemented` is a **per-unit** post of `/implement`'s route step. An ask whose work landed
through a session working it directly therefore reaches no route step at all: its thread ends at
the `📥 受理` receipt, and from the channel an ask that shipped three hours ago and one nobody has
started are byte-identical. Measured 2026-09-02: three merged pull requests, the issue closed, and
the operator found out by asking a session.

**Highlights:**

1. A reader at the **item grain** — `propose/scripts/list-unannounced-closed-asks.sh` — names this
   repository's finished asks from the repository and the issues alone, never a channel scan, and
   carries what closed each one.
2. The `/infinite-development` tick replies one finish line into each item's own thread. The dedup
   is **the thread itself**: no ledger, no cursor, no field on any artifact.
3. Where it cannot see, it says nothing and says why. A blind read is never rendered as *nothing to
   announce* — a collapse this repository has twice measured and refused.

## 2. Motivation

The neighbouring reader was checked before anything was designed for it.
`moderate/scripts/reconcile-candidates.sh` lists `repos/<slug>/pulls` and keeps only heads matching
`work-*` — the claim protocol's own branch pattern — so issue #917, closed 2026-09-02 with its work
merged, is named by no candidate and no `unresolved` row of a `--window-days 3 --limit 20` run. The
excluded term is the branch pattern, and the grain is the whole difference: that reader answers
*which pull request*, this one answers *which item*.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. The reader (`list-unannounced-closed-asks.sh`)

One listing call decides the candidate set: closed issues through `gh-rest.sh`, kept when the body
carries the sweep's `slack-ref:` marker **or** when a feedback record on the base names the issue's
`/issues/<N>` URL. Each is resolved to its feedback stem — the token a thread root carries as
`fb:<stem>` — and a candidate with no resolvable stem lands in `unresolved` under
`stems_unresolvable` rather than being dropped or given an invented key.

### 3-2. What landed

Per candidate: one timeline read, plus one single-pull GET for each merged cross-reference, that
second capped by `WORKAHOLIC_ANNOUNCE_LANDED_MAX` (default 5) and reporting `landed_truncated`.
The whole read count stays a function of `--limit`. `landed[]` carries `{number, title, url,
merged_by, merged_at}`; an unmerged cross-reference is filtered in the jq program, because a pull
request that mentioned the issue and was closed did not close it.

`closed_unmerged` is claimed only on a **positive** reading — the timeline was read and named no
merged pull request. An unreadable timeline answers `landed_read: timeline_unreadable` with
`closed_unmerged: false`, so *nobody merged anything* and *I could not see what merged* never read
alike.

### 3-3. The shape, and the act

The finish line reuses `🟢 Implemented`, marked by its sentence rather than a fifth colour — the
precedent `thread-reconcile` set for a merged item announced late. It lives byte-identically in
`notify/reference/notifications.md` and `commands/infinite-development.md`, with its bounds stated
beside it: no mention token, a reply and never a root, once ever per item, the connector as the
only transport that can resolve the thread at all, and an unresolvable field stated as unresolved.

The step runs after the channel turn and before any subagent is spawned. Outcomes are a closed set:
`announced` / `already_announced` / `thread_unresolved: <reason>` / `post_failed: <reason>` /
`held: <reason>`, with `no_candidates` for an idle tick — and naming a candidate without an outcome
is non-conformant on its face.

### 3-4. The pins and the drill

`testOneSessionLoop` asserts the two copies byte-identical, the five bounds present, the refusal to
mint a colour, and the outcome vocabulary in **both** directions — every word the tick may report
is named in the ceiling, and every reason word the reader emits is documented in the reader's own
header. `scripts/e2e/loop-drill.sh verify-announced-asks` drills the mechanical half offline (8
load-bearing rows) with a **breaker**: wire the refusal to answer with an empty candidate list, and
a blind hour becomes indistinguishable from a quiet one.

landed and by whom, however the work reached `main`. It is posted once and never repeated, an idle
tick posts nothing, and a reading the tick could not make leaves the thread alone.

## 5. Concerns

- **The post itself is checkable by nothing.** The pins reach the shape and the reader; whether a
  run actually posts, and whether it holds its tongue where it should, is not mechanically
  verifiable — the same limit this repository already states about the Japanese rule. What the
  breaker buys is that the *reader's* blindness distinction cannot silently regress.
  Severity: low.
- **The tick's read budget grew.** The candidate set still costs one listing call, but what landed
  costs one timeline read per candidate plus one GET per merged cross-reference. It is bounded by
  `--limit` and by `WORKAHOLIC_ANNOUNCE_LANDED_MAX`, and the tick runs every five minutes — worth
  watching if the default limit is ever raised. Severity: low.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- **A `gh` stub that prints response JSON does not stand in for `gh api --jq`.** The transport
  applies the program before the caller sees bytes, so a raw-JSON stub exercises a shape the real
  transport never produces — and it fails in a way that looks like a reader bug. The stub here
  reads the `--jq` argument off its own argv and pipes the fixture through `jq -r`; where one
  endpoint of several must fail, the dispatcher lives in its own file, because chaining through
  `sh -c "<body>"` lets the outer shell expand `$1` and `$prog` first.
- **Byte-identity across two files is broken by the words that read naturally in place.** "the
  precedent set **above**" is true in the catalog and false in the command, and a cross-reference
  naming *the other file* differs by construction. The shared region has to be written
  positionless, naming both files in both copies.
- **Pin a vocabulary in the direction nobody writes first.** Asserting the *reader's* reason words
  appear in its own header caught `stems_unresolvable` — a word the script emitted and no document
  named, which an operator would have met for the first time in a failure.
- **Two nested blindness surfaces need two words.** The listing decides whether there are
  candidates at all; the per-candidate timeline decides what closed each one. Collapsing them would
  let a single unreadable timeline silence an hour that had other announceable items in it.
