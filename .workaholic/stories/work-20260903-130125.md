## 1. Overview

The `🔎 Moderation` thread is read by a developer deciding what to do next, and it read as a bot
reporting its own internals. Seven tickets, every one measured against one morning's actual thread.

**Highlights:**

1. **One question per kind.** A step now hands back `groups` — one entry per reading, naming every
   subject — so three arrived directions cost one reply instead of three near-identical ones.
2. **The bodies carry the act and nothing else.** The tick's own counters (`It would leave N
   unreached and M unclaimed.`) and a repository-wide residue fact are gone from the questions they
   were not about, and the `arrived` body now states a reading rather than offering a bare choice.
3. **A closed list of what a rendered post never carries**, stated where the composition happens —
   including the promise that had been made twice in one morning and kept by nobody.

## 2. Motivation

Every item is a measurement from one morning, not a preference:

- **five `🙋` in twenty-four seconds**, three of them the same sentence with a direction slug
  swapped. `lib/question-id.sh` keys per subject, so N candidates cost N questions and the per-tick
  cap only spaced them out.
- `It would leave N unreached and M unclaimed.` at the head of **every** body — the tick saying what
  its counters would hold afterwards, to a person who did not ask.
- the unattributed residue pasted into **all five** headings because the composer had it in hand.
- the `arrived` question saying a count and asking a bare choice, linking nothing.
- a printed `tick-day:20260903` and a sentence explaining which internal step would have handled
  something the tick decided not to say.
- the same three `⚠️` lines on the root and again, verbatim, in the digest **thirty-eight minutes
  later** — with `base-health` unable to read the base's checks arriving as a digest's fourth bullet.
- *the next `[Implement]` tick will try to resolve each conflict itself*, said twice in one morning
  and true on none of them, because those units were excluded from claiming.

## 3. What changed

`step-direction-health.sh` gained `groups`, lost `$leaving_clause`, and narrowed the residue to the
two readings it is a fact about. `commands/moderate.md` — the surface where the agent turns a step
payload into a message — gained the closed list, the group rule and the impairment rule. Five step
scripts lost the promise; a grep for it now returns zero.

## 4. Concerns

**The group key carries the sorted subject set, not the kind alone.** A bare `direction-arrived` key
would be asked once ever, so a direction arriving next week would never be asked about at all — the
asked-once gate turned into a silence, which is worse than the defect. The stated cost is that a
fourth direction joining an already-asked group re-asks that whole group once.

**The residue stays on `arrived`.** Deleting it everywhere would reopen the 2026-08-28 mission
`say-what-the-direction-could-not-see-before-calling-it-arrived`, whose whole point is that nobody is
asked to call a direction finished without seeing what the loop could not attribute. The ticket's own
test — *the residue belongs where it is a fact about the subject* — is what decides it.

**Nothing mechanical can see what a session emits.** The suite proves the rules are present in the
ceiling the session reads, and each assertion says so in its own comment rather than implying more.

**These seven tickets carried no `## Key Files`, `## Implementation Steps` or `## Quality Gate`** —
only an `## Overview` and `## Policies`, which is below `validate-ticket.sh`'s own floor for a queued
ticket. Each Overview was specific enough to drive from, and each was driven; the gap is recorded
here rather than absorbed silently, because a ticket set emitted below the floor will happen again.

## 5. Successful development patterns

**An apostrophe in a jq comment breaks the script.** These steps carry jq programs inside
single-quoted shell strings, so writing *the tick's own bookkeeping* in a comment terminated the
quote and broke `sh -n`. The block now says so in its own last line, because the next person editing
it will reach for an apostrophe too.

**A promise is worth checking against what the speaker can derive.** *The next `[Implement]` tick
attempts each* was not merely optimistic — whether a unit is offered is `plan-units.sh`'s answer, and
no `/moderate` step may reach it. Naming **whose act it is** and never **when** is what a step can
actually say, and it is the general form of the fix.

**Six suite rows pinned the exact behaviour being retired.** That is the mechanism working, and each
was rewritten to be strictly more specific than what it replaced: two inverted to assert the
residue's absence, two rewritten to the report contract, and two to a body whose whole text is the
act. A retirement that costs nothing to prove is a retirement nothing was guarding.
