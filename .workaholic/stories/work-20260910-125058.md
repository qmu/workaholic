## 1. Overview

Both queued tickets asked for mechanisms that had already landed under two differently-named
missions. This branch verified every acceptance criterion against the tree by fixture, found no
gap, and recorded the one place the documentation disagrees with the code.

**Highlights:**

1. Both tickets' criteria proved against throwaway roots and the live scripts, not against test titles.
2. No line of `transport/` or `workaholify/` changed — the finding was that nothing needed changing.
3. `CLAUDE.md` groups `qfs_preview_refused` with the fallback-permitting classes while the classifier maps it to `none`; minted as a ticket.

## 2. Motivation

Two tickets sat in `todo/` naming a mission closed under a different slug, so the survey offered
them as loose backlog long after their substance merged. The repository forbids retiring a ticket
a run judges mooted — a reading about behaviour, not a file test — so the sanctioned way such a
ticket leaves the queue is to drive it: verify what it asked for, and archive it with the
evidence. A ticket whose mechanism is assumed present is one nobody ever checked, and that
assumption is exactly what a closed-mission handover loses.

- A ticket whose mission closed under another slug is verified against the tree by fixture before any gap is assumed — running the scripts answered in minutes what reading them could only suggest.
