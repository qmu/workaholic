## 1. Overview

Two readings the `/moderate` tick hands a person were repaired: a question it raised could
disappear before anybody heard it, and the number it reports as owed counted steps rather than
decisions and named none of them. Both were reproduced into a fixture before anything changed.

**Highlights:**

1. The registry persists a raised question independently of its step's window, and the check-in drains from it as well as from the log.
2. `reconcile-questions.sh` registers the run report's own question keys, so the record no longer depends on the agent writing a per-key line.
3. `render` joins the classification table, `strategy-digest` moves to it, and `left_steps` names the rows the count is made of.

## 2. Motivation

Both defects made a tick look healthier than it was. Measured 2026-09-17, one `questions-held`
line named four keys and promised each "is offered again"; one got the per-key log line the
arrears rode, and it is the only one still reachable. And four ticks reported `N left to a person`
as 2, 2, 3 and 3 naming nobody — one of the three was `strategy-digest`, a render.

Each mirrors a repair already on the base. #1200 stopped a question being **retired** on an
absence of a reading; the first ticket closes the other end of that pipe, where a question
**evaporates** because its step moved on. The table's default — unclassified means
`needs_ruling` — put a render into a count of decisions.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Keep a raised question askable past its window ([d0f29157f](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/d0f29157f))

The registry became a candidate source of its own: `step-human-checkin.sh` drains the union of the
log's `human-checkin-held-*` slugs and every registry row in state `candidate` or `asked`, deduped
on the content key, each entry naming `source` and `first_seen`. `reconcile-questions.sh` registers
every key the run report carries under the step that raised it — which also repairs the measured
`step: direction-health`-for-every-key rows — and `register` writes `first_seen` once, on creation.

### 3-2. Count decisions, not steps, left to a person ([8ff3d201d](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/8ff3d201d))

`render` is a third classification word in the one table and `strategy-digest` moved to it, the only
row that moved. `step-file-findings.sh` reads that word exactly as it reads `repairable`, excludes a
`render` row that merely reported, keeps any row that reported `degraded` or `blocked` whatever its
class, and names the count's members in `left_steps` and in its summary.

## 4. Outcome

The measured hour answers `left: 2` naming both members; the measured registry row is offered
`source: "registry"`. No gate, cap, window, refusal word or filing rule moved, and no new script.

## 5. Concerns

### An unasked candidate is now offered until it is asked once

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** A registry row stays in the arrears until an ask is recorded, so a question whose premise resolved before anybody heard it will still be asked once (see [d0f29157f](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/d0f29157f) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/moderate/scripts/step-human-checkin.sh`). The ticket names this cost and accepts it: asking once beats extinguishing a raised question, which is #1200's own ruling one layer up.
- **How to Fix:** Nothing, unless it measurably becomes noise — the repair would be a step emitting `resolved_keys`, which retires the row positively through the path that already exists.

### Three legacy log slugs remain unidentifiable

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** `direction-expiring-an-au-16258524`, `stranded-unit-retire-a-c-731715597` and `stuck-634714808-3638566254` were held on 2026-09-02 before the registry existed and have no recoverable preimage, so they are offered every tick as `question_identity_unavailable` (see [d0f29157f](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/d0f29157f) in `plugins/workaholic/skills/moderate/reference/question-lifecycle.md`). That information loss is already realised and no key may be fabricated to clear it.
- **How to Fix:** The operator deletes the three log lines, or leaves them visible; the loop may not invent a key for them.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Seeding the persisted record in the shape the **old** code wrote found a defect a fresh fixture could not: `list` dropped each row's map key, which only matters for a legacy row, and the reader then reported an outcome for a question named by the literal string `"null"`.
- Reproducing each defect into a throwaway fixture *before* editing gave both tickets a falsifiable before/after — `left: 3` → `left: 2`, and `held_count: 0` over a live `candidate` row → offered with its source.
