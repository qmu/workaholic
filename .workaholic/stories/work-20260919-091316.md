## 1. Overview

Two mechanisms the loop relies on had quietly stopped being reachable: `/fb` read its assignee from
a field that has been empty since August, and the worktree reaper had no caller at all. Both were
the operator's own reports (issues #1213 and #1212), and both are now readable from the outside —
the asks the inbox declines are named, and the worktrees it holds are counted with their reasons.

**Highlights:**

1. `/fb` reads `gh api user --jq .login` and refuses `identity_unresolved` rather than filing an
   unassigned issue nobody's discovery lists.
2. `list-inbound-issues.sh` reports every open unassigned issue under the new reason word
   `unassigned` — visibility only; the offer is byte-identical.
3. `/moderate`'s new `worktree-sweep` step gives `reap-worktrees.sh` its first caller in the six
   weeks since it was written.

## 2. Motivation

`gh-rest.sh available` moved to probing `GET /rate_limit` on 2026-08-29, which emptied its `login`
field; four scripts were migrated to `gh api user` and the two `/fb` documents were not, because
`/fb`'s caller is an agent reading prose rather than a script. The consequence was worse than a
stale sentence: `list-inbound-issues.sh` filters server-side on `assignee=<login>`, so an
unassigned issue was returned in **neither** `issues[]` nor `excluded[]` — omitted rather than
excluded. Measured 2026-09-19, nine open issues on this repository carried `assignees: []`, every
one authored by the operator, two of them the reports of this very defect. Separately,
`reap-worktrees.sh` was built after a 53 GB incident and never given a caller: outside its own body
it appeared only in two documentation rows and two test fixtures, so the one script written to
catch what teardown structurally cannot ran when a person typed it and never otherwise.

Both findings share a shape this repository has repaired before: a reading that cannot distinguish
*nothing here* from *nothing I could see*. `backlog_all_excluded` was the same fix for the drive
survey, and `superseded`'s move from ancestry to a tree reading (issue #788) is the same correction
the reaper's permanently-`false` `merged` term still carries.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Read the fb assignee from gh api user ([c77cce915](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c77cce915))

`feedback/SKILL.md` step 3 and `feedback/reference/crossing.md` step 6 now name `gh api user --jq
.login` — the same call the reader makes, so writer and reader agree about who the person is by
construction — and state the `identity_unresolved` refusal, which files nothing and deliberately
never takes *The fallback*. `list-inbound-issues.sh` makes one further repository-scoped read
(`assignee=none`) and reports each open, non-pull-request issue in `excluded[]` under the new
reason word `unassigned`; a failure there warns on stderr and never turns a readable inbox into
`list_failed`. A one-time sweep assigned seven already-unassigned issues to their own authors.

### 3-2. Give the worktree reaper a moderate caller ([dced22645](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/dced22645))

`moderate/scripts/step-worktree-sweep.sh` runs `reap-worktrees.sh --apply` hourly from the main
checkout, registered in `steps.json` beside `retire-claims` and documented as §33. It derives no
predicate of its own and passes no survey in — the reaper re-derives the proof from a fresh survey
at the moment of the act — and its summary names the held backlog with its skip-reason breakdown,
byte totals excluded so an unchanged backlog renders no hourly root line.

## 4. Outcome

The two readings that were silent now speak: an ask the inbox declines is named with its reason,
and a worktree the sweep holds is counted under the word that holds it. Neither routing decision
moved — unassigned issues are still not offered, and `reclaimable` is still `merged AND clean` —
which is what let both repairs ship without re-opening a recorded ruling.

## 5. Concerns

### The first worktree sweep on this repository frees nothing

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** `merged` is `ahead == 0` against `origin/<base>`, an ancestry test, and every
  pull request this loop merges is squash-merged — so a landed branch is permanently
  `merged: false` and the four standing worktrees (191.7 M) will read `unmerged` forever (see
  [dced22645](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/dced22645) in
  `plugins/workaholic/skills/branching/scripts/survey-worktrees.sh`)
- **How to Fix:** Nothing here — the operator ruled on 2026-09-19 that the predicate is right and
  is not to be loosened. What changed is that the backlog is now reported rather than invisible;
  whether a tree-based landing proof should replace the ancestry test is a separate ask.

### The unassigned sweep changes one person's queue depth

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** step 7 routed seven issues into the operator's own hourly `[Specificate]` queue
  at once, which is the correct destination and a visible change (see
  [c77cce915](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c77cce915) in
  `plugins/workaholic/skills/specificate/scripts/list-inbound-issues.sh`)
- **How to Fix:** Nothing mechanical. The discovery tick takes them oldest-first, and the two the
  sweep deliberately skipped (#1212, #1213) are already captured as feedback records.

### Issue #1212's third finding has no ticket

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** `retire-claim.sh` inspects only `${repo_root}/.worktrees/${unit}` and reports
  `absent` as a success, so a worktree whose directory name is not the unit slug has no teardown
  owner at all (see [dced22645](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/dced22645) in
  `plugins/workaholic/skills/drive/scripts/retire-claim.sh`)
- **How to Fix:** Resolve the worktree by its branch rather than its directory name. It is a
  different script with a different repair and was deliberately left out of scope; it still needs
  its own ticket.

### The unassigned listing costs one REST call per discovery tick

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** every `/specificate` discovery now makes a second repository-scoped listing, and
  a repository whose maintainers deliberately leave issues unassigned will carry a standing
  non-empty `excluded[]` (see [c77cce915](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c77cce915) in
  `plugins/workaholic/skills/specificate/scripts/list-inbound-issues.sh`)
- **How to Fix:** Accepted cost, stated in the script's own header. The read is strictly advisory —
  it runs after the inbox is already served and its failure is warned, never fatal.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns
