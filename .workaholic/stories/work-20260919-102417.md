## 1. Overview

A person's feedback issue closed when the **proposal** merged, and the reconciliation deciding
whether anything landed compared an expected review surface no artifact carried. This branch
persists that surface, removes the closing keyword from the ingest seam, and adds the one act
that may close a source issue.

**Highlights:**

1. `review_surface:` is written by `create.sh --review-surface` and read back; a
   caller-supplied `expected_surface` is now ignored.
2. The ingest publication writes `Refs #<N>` and says the issue stays open until reconciled.
3. `close-source-issue.sh` closes only on `implemented_and_verified`.

## 2. Motivation

A tree-wide walk found `expected_surface` in exactly two readers, in both as an input, carried
by no artifact — so both sides of the comparison were written by the party the comparison exists
to check. Measured on a consuming repository: a request about a public prototype screen was
implemented in the application package's shell, the item was closed, and the summary claimed it
landed. The closure itself was decided at the wrong end of the pipeline: `Closes #<N>` on the
ingest body ended the ask before any implementation, verification or surface existed.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Persist the ask's review surface on its record ([c39c549b6](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c39c549b6))

The review surface now lives on the feedback record — the grain the reader reconciles, with one
writer and immutable by design, so the party implementing the work cannot edit the expectation it
is judged against. `feedback-outcome.sh` resolves it per item and `delivery-ledger.sh` composes
that one reader; an absent surface stays `surface_unresolved` and an absent record answers a new
`surface_unreadable` with its own reason.

### 3-2. Reference the ask's issue instead of closing it ([e91adb39f](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/e91adb39f))

The ingest caller sets `WORKAHOLIC_REFERENCES_ISSUE` instead of `WORKAHOLIC_CLOSES_ISSUE`, so the
body carries a plain `Refs #<N>` and states that the issue stays open until the work is
reconciled. The re-offer exclusion was established first: it keys on a record naming the issue's
URL, never on closure, so leaving the issue open re-offers nothing.

### 3-3. Close a source issue only on a verified verdict ([182c0e00d](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/182c0e00d))

`close-source-issue.sh` re-derives the verdict through the same one reader in the same invocation
as the close, closes only on `implemented_and_verified`, answers `already_closed` on a repeat,
and refuses every other state by that state's own word with no request made. It is wired at
`/implement`'s per-item reconciliation and nowhere else.

## 4. Outcome

A feedback item ends when evidence from the surface the person named says it landed, and stays
open with a named state when it did not — one writer, one reader, one actor.

## 5. Concerns

### Most items will now reconcile `surface_unresolved` and never close

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** an ask naming no review surface can never reach `implemented_and_verified`
  (see [c39c549b6](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/c39c549b6) in
  `skills/work/scripts/feedback-outcome.sh`) — already true, but it now decides closure.
- **How to Fix:** decide as its own ask whether such an item is closable on the remaining
  evidence; a pass here would reopen the hole this branch closed.

### An ask whose work lands outside `/implement` reaches no closer

- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** the close is wired at `/implement`'s per-item reconciliation only (see
  [182c0e00d](https://github.com/qmu/workaholic/commit/182c0e00d) in
  `commands/implement.md`), the same gap the announcement already had.
- **How to Fix:** a person closes it by hand; a sweep hunting for work to declare finished is
  what the ticket forbids.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- A reader belongs in the skill that owns the artifact it reads: the generated bundle's closure
  follows references, so ownership and closure cost point the same way.
- Establishing the re-offer exclusion's actual key before removing the close cost one file read
  and decided whether the ticket could proceed at all.
