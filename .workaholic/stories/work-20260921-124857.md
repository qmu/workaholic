## 1. Overview

The Slack incident gate had a reader and no writer. `verify-live-proof.sh` refuses closure unless an evidence document proves every declared operation, the sender and one root/reply/reaction round trip — and nothing in the tree produced such a document, so its shape was described only inside that script's own jq program and the operator's remaining act was "save the typed evidence" into a format nobody had written down. This adds the writer.

## 2. Motivation

A gate whose input is hand-made is a gate nobody can check. The ticket's own implementation step 4 asks for *durable per-operation proof* beside the closure offer, and only the offer existed.

The writer is deliberately separate from the gate and deliberately weaker than it. A route that **advertises** an operation has a capability; the gate asks whether the operation was **performed**. Reading the first as the second is `operations_unsatisfied` dressed as delivery, which the ticket's own quality gate forbids by name — so `available` carries the capability reading and `proved` stays `false` on every row until a live act fills it in. A describe nobody could make answers `available: null`, never `false`: `false` means the route answered and does not carry the operation, and an absence of a reading is never a verdict.
